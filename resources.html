Comparisons

Two groups, and what separates them.

Every comparison below names the two groups and the question it answers. Three we have run on public discussion. Three we would run. We say which is which.

  • Latest signals

We read reviews, calls and open text and compare what two groups say. The words that show up disproportionately in one and not the other are the finding. Everything below is that method applied to a public question.

  • Compare two groups, not one
  • Report the difference, with the evidence behind it
  • Say what it would change
