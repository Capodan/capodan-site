HOW IT WORKS

From real conversations to a change you can make.

  1. 01

    Capture

    We collect thousands of real conversations from public sources, or work with your own reviews, calls and support tickets.

  2. 02

    Refine

    We strip out anything that isn't a real customer talking, such as brand replies, duplicates, bots and adverts.

  3. 03

    Compare

    We test every word and phrase to see what one group says far more than the other, and what is just noise.

  4. 04

    Read

    A linguist reads the conversations behind each difference that holds up, to find the reason underneath it.

  5. 05

    Apply

    We set each finding against how you sell now, and turn the gaps into changes your team can make.

The numbers find the patterns. The reading explains them. If we can't show where a finding came from, it doesn't go in.

FINDINGS

What the comparison turned up.

Two comparisons we ran on public conversations. They're demonstrations, not client results.

Families choosing a community vs families living in one

Families still choosing have open questions about staff, care and fees.

3.85× more likely to ask questions than families already living in one. Staff, medical cover and extra fees come up most.

"there were many other random and monthly charges that were not mentioned when we toured"

Where it costs youThese questions sit inside the decision itself. If your tours don't surface and answer them early, families are making a high-value decision with key concerns still open.

Jaguar owners vs Land Rover, Audi and Infiniti owners

Jaguar owners value how the car makes them look. Rival owners rarely mention it.

17.3× more likely to talk about how the brand comes across

"a brilliant modern sport saloon with nothing old man about it at all"

Where it costs youIf your sales story leads with spec, you're competing on ground every rival can also claim. How the car makes its owner look is what Jaguar owners value and rival owners rarely raise.

COMMON QUESTIONS

What do you compare?

Groups of customers tied to a real decision. Buyers and people who walked away, customers who stay and customers who leave, your market and a competitor's.

Where does the language come from?

Public conversations such as forums, reviews and discussions, in the languages your customers speak. Nothing private is used and names are never published.

Can you use our own data?

Yes. Reviews, call transcripts, support tickets and sales notes can be compared the same way, and usually give the sharpest answer.

What do we get at the end?

The findings, the real customer conversations behind each one, and a short list of changes your team can make.
