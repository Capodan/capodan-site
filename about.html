Why Capodan

We turn unstructured language into decisions you can act on. Built on linguistic expertise, delivered with commercial clarity.

  • Linguistics-first
  • Strategy-ready outputs
  • Work with global teams

Define two groups

Buyers and non-buyers. Retained and churned. Your market and a competitor's. The comparison has to matter commercially.

  • Reviews and calls
  • Surveys and open text
  • Competitor and category language

Measure the difference

We find what shows up disproportionately in one group versus the other. Frequency, likelihood, and the words people actually use.

  • Keyness and frequency evidence
  • Concordance and collocation
  • Contamination checks before anything is reported

Turn it into a decision

The finding, the evidence behind it, and what to change.

  • Prioritised actions
  • Messaging and objection handling
  • What to test next

Our purpose

What people say isn’t always what they mean. We surface the gap and show how to use it. For over a decade we’ve worked at the intersection of communication and strategy, helping teams act on what truly drives decisions.

  • Sharper diagnosis of customer reality
  • Clear framing that shifts perception
  • Faster consensus across teams

Why we’re different

Most work in this space describes one group. We compare two, and measure what separates them. That difference is harder to argue with than an opinion about what customers want, and it points at something specific to change.

Method

Evidence-led linguistic analysis paired with practical templates.

Outcomes

Cleaner propositions, fewer objections, faster adoption.
